# Confirmed production input unit standard

Owner confirmation: 19 October 2026.

For NDAYENI, SIVANA and BIRCH_ACRES:
- RAW-013 Bacon: **1 EA = 1 individual slice**.
- RAW-012 Ham: **1 EA = 1 individual slice**.
- Purchases, transfers, stock counts and menu recipes must be expressed in slices.
- Example: receiving 20 bacon slices = +20 EA; using 1 bacon slice in a meal = -1 EA.
- Do not reinterpret historical decimal quantities as slice counts without verifying their source and original measurement unit.
- Proposed implementation remains development-only. No production recipe, balance or receipt changes are authorised by this documentation.
- Validate recipes and perform a controlled migration only after verified database backup and approved tests.
